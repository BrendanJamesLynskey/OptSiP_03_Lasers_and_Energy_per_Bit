# Silicon Photonics Fundamentals 03 — Lasers, Packaging and the Energy-per-Bit Budget

Why silicon borrows its laser and the four ways to bring one; external laser modules, wall-plug efficiency and comb sources; fibre attach and packaging; putting electronics next to photonics; and the link budget from laser to detector, priced in picojoules per bit, with what published links actually report.

Topics: Laser integration, ELSFP, Wall-plug efficiency, Packaging, Link budget, pJ/bit.

**Live site:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/OptSiP_03_Lasers_and_Energy_per_Bit/

Part of the [Silicon Photonics Fundamentals series](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/Optical_Hub_Silicon_Photonics).
